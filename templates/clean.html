Proposed progression · SDU · Teacher review required

Start with one connection

For a manageable pilot, agree what students do in the earlier course, what the next course can expect, and which support remains available. The sampled FA503 → FA504 correction is one candidate. An explicit link, a student-written adaptation and one check could be enough for a first change.

How to use this proposal

A sequence of expectations

Introduce, practise, extend, transfer and integrate describe the purpose of an encounter. They are not proficiency scores. Reference material and appropriate support remain available.

Different timing

Computing, data handling and plotting start early. Governance grows around relevant decisions. Machine learning starts later with critical appraisal; training complex models is not a core expectation.

Compare with actual teaching

Record the student task, support, participation and evidence in the workbook’s Review sheet. Not reviewed means unknown. Recorded category content alone does not establish alignment with a target.

Check the change when it runs

Agree one exercise change with teachers, then inspect student work. Agreement on a curriculum expectation does not establish that students have attained it. Projects can use a common case where comparable opportunities are missing.

How to read curriculum progression

Independence

Is the implementation supplied, completed within a guide, or planned by students?

Judgement

Are operations prescribed, choices bounded, or methods and limitations openly evaluated?

Transfer

Does the task match an example, explicitly recall an earlier method, or require students to recognise the connection themselves?

What remains to be checked

Teachers need to confirm required participation, available help, and expected outputs. Later semesters need not increase every demand. These are curriculum expectations, not measured student attainment.

# Teacher review of the progression pilot

Review the activities actually used in FA515, FA503, and FA504. The starting interpretations are in [evidence.csv](../assets/data/evidence.csv); the [pilot guide](README.md) defines the descriptors. No student work or attainment data is needed.

## Review with each teacher

Allow approximately 20–30 minutes per course. For each sampled activity, establish:

1. Which teaching year and student group use this version? Is it used, required, optional, or no longer used?
2. What must students produce? Which parts are completed individually, in groups, or demonstrated by the teacher?
3. What code, worked examples, hints, solutions, and other assistance are available when students do the task?
4. Which calculations and decisions must students make themselves? Is that expectation checked through an exercise, report, or other course requirement?
5. Which earlier activity is explicitly assumed or recalled? Can students follow the guide without using that earlier learning?
6. Do the three descriptors fit? Add the relevant assignment instructions or explanation where the public guide is incomplete.

Confirm how assessment is intended to work, without making claims about how well students perform. Link only shareable supporting material here; a teacher's description can document requirements held in itslearning.

## Joint comparison

Allow approximately 45 minutes after the course reviews. Compare the actual task requirements, not course titles or semester numbers.

| Comparison | Question | Initial status |
|---|---|---|
| `503-D-P` → `504-C-P` | Does sample-removal correction move from supplied code to student implementation in a new context? | Candidate; confirm support and required participation. |
| `515-K-M` → `504-A-M` | How are kinetics and Arrhenius analysis reused, and what additional expectation is placed on students? | Candidate; confirm the teaching connection. |
| `515-K-M` → `504-C-M` | How do model choice and fitting-interval choice develop students' judgement? | Compare different decisions; do not assume an increase. |
| FA503 required modelling | Does another required activity connect modelling across all three courses? | Unresolved; the sampled Weibull fit is optional. |

For each comparison, record **increased demand**, **consolidation**, **different demand**, or **insufficient evidence**, identifying the dimension and the reason. A later exercise need not increase all three dimensions.
